A degree holder of Peace and Governance at Bindura University, Panashe Chijokwe is thoroughly equipped with cutting-edge peace and conflict resolution strategies. Above all earthly achievements, she remains the cherished daughter of the Chijokwe family, holding the foundational responsibility of being the first-born in a family of three. Anchoring her academic, national, and spiritual pillars, understanding and smiling stand out beautifully as her finest natural skills.
